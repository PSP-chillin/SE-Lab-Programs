SE LAB 02: Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira
Project topic: Hostel Maintenance & Issue Ticketing System


PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------

Use Jira to turn the functional requirements from Lab 1 into Agile backlog
items. Then simulate a sprint and analyse the progress.

Learning outcomes covered:
  - Create Epics and User Stories in Jira using Agile conventions
  - Prioritise backlog items by business value and user needs
  - Estimate effort with Story Points (Fibonacci sequence)
  - Apply Planning Poker as a collaborative estimation technique
  - Simulate a sprint (To Do -> In Progress -> Done)
  - Track progress with Jira's Burndown Chart
  - Reflect on team performance and the Agile workflow

Deliverables asked for in the handout:
  - Screenshots of the backlog with Epics and User Stories, the story
    point assignments, the active sprint board, and the burndown chart
  - A short document answering the reflection questions
