SE LAB 01: Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------
Hostel Maintenance & Issue Ticketing System

Allowing students to solve their issue with the Maintenance staff and the hostel administration, but they needs three
things: An issue reporting and tracking mechanism, real-time communication and status updates, and structured maintenance resolution and feedback recording.

Stakeholders / Actors: Hostellers/students, Warden, Maintenance staff, Hostel Administrators, system Administrator. 

Deliverables asked for in the handout:
  1. A requirements table with exactly 5 Functional Requirements
     (FR-001 to FR-005) and 2 Non-Functional Requirements (NFR-001 and
     NFR-002). Each has ID, Type, Description, Priority, Acceptance
     Criteria and Rationale.
  2. A UML use-case diagram that models all actors and primary use cases,
     with at least one <<include>> and one <<extend>> relationship.
  3. A one-page use-case flow specification for one core use case, with
     preconditions, postconditions, a main success scenario and one
     alternate flow.
